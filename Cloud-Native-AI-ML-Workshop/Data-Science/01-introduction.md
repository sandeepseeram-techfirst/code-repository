### Data Science 

**Data science** is an interdisciplinary field that uses statistical methods, computational algorithms, and domain expertise to extract meaningful insights, patterns, and predictions from structured and unstructured data. It transforms raw data into actionable decisions across industries, from fraud detection in banking to recommendation algorithms in streaming platforms.

### The Core Pillars

**Computer Science & Software Engineering:** Writing scalable code (Python, R, SQL) to scrape, clean, process, and query vast datasets, as well as deploying models into production environments.

**Mathematics & Statistics:** Applying probability distributions, linear algebra, calculus, and statistical hypothesis testing to model data behavior accurately and avoid false correlations.

**Domain & Business Knowledge:** Understanding the specific operational context (e.g., healthcare diagnostics, retail supply chain) to formulate the right questions and translate model metrics into real-world business value. 

### How Data Science Works: The End-to-End Lifecycle

**Data science operates as an iterative lifecycle that takes raw, messy inputs and converts them into deployed solutions.**
