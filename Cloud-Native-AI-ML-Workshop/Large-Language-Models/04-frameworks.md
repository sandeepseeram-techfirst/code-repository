## Frameworks 

**Choosing the right framework for language model development is a crucial step in building a successful AI-powered application.**  

### TensorFlow:
TensorFlow is an open-source software library developed by Google for machine learning. It has a large community of developers and researchers, which means there are many resources available for learning and troubleshooting. 

TensorFlow provides a simple and flexible platform for building and training neural networks, and it supports both CPU and GPU computations.
